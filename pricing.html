Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Why are you so transparent about pricing?
A: Because we're building FATHOM in public. Hiding pricing creates friction, wastes time, and benefits no one. If our pricing works for you, great. If not, no hard feelings.
Q: Can we negotiate pricing?
A: Our pricing is fair and based on real costs. We don't inflate prices to negotiate down. What you see is what you pay. That said, we can customize packages for unique situations—just ask.
Q: What if we need to pause or cancel?
A: We require 3-month minimum commitments (SEO takes time). After that, you can cancel with 30 days' notice. No hidden fees, no hard sell to stay.
Q: Do you offer payment plans?
A: Yes. We accept monthly payments (no upfront lump sum). For annual commitments, we offer quarterly payments at a 5% discount.
Q: How do you compare to other agencies?
A: We're typically 20-30% more expensive than offshore agencies, but 30-40% cheaper than large brand-name agencies. You get senior-level strategy at mid-tier pricing.
Q: What if we don't see results?
A: SEO takes 3-6 months minimum. If after 6 months you haven't seen meaningful traffic growth (100%+), we'll either extend your contract at 50% discount or part ways amicably. We stand behind our work.
