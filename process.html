How We Work:
The FATHOM Method

Most agencies keep their process vague. At FATHOM, we've engineered a systematic approach to SEO that's repeatable, transparent, and results-driven. You'll know exactly what's happening, why it's happening, and what outcomes to expect.

Here's how we take you from invisible to inevitable.

The 7 Phases of Strategic SEO

01
Weeks 1-2

Discovery & Strategic Audit

Timeline: 1-2 weeks

Deep-dive into your business model, revenue drivers, and growth goals. Comprehensive technical audit, content & authority analysis, and competitive intelligence.

What We Do

  • Business Intelligence: Deep-dive into your business model, revenue drivers, ideal customer profile, and competitive landscape
  • Technical Audit: Crawl your entire site, analyze Core Web Vitals, identify crawlability issues, review site architecture
  • Content & Authority Audit: Analyze existing content quality, identify content gaps, review backlink profile for quality and opportunities
  • Competitive Benchmarking: Analyze top 5 competitors, identify their strengths and weaknesses

Deliverables

  • Comprehensive audit report (30-50 pages)
  • Prioritized opportunity analysis (quick wins + long-term plays)
  • Competitive intelligence overview
  • Technical issues log with severity ratings
02
Week 2-3

Strategic Roadmap & Planning

Timeline: 1 week

Build your 12-month SEO strategy with clear objectives, prioritized initiatives, content strategy mapping, link building approach, and measurement framework.

What We Do

  • 12-Month SEO Strategy: Define clear objectives, prioritize initiatives based on impact and effort, map content strategy with topic clusters
  • Tactical Execution Plan: Month-by-month milestones, week-by-week content calendar (first 90 days detailed)
  • Technical Optimization Sequence: Foundation → optimization → scaling
  • Success Metrics & KPIs: Establish measurement framework, custom dashboard setup

Deliverables

  • 12-month strategic roadmap
  • 90-day detailed execution plan
  • Content editorial calendar (first 30 days scheduled)
  • Success metrics dashboard (custom setup)
03
Weeks 3-6

Technical Foundation

Timeline: 3-4 weeks

Resolve critical technical fixes, optimize Core Web Vitals, implement schema markup, and improve site architecture for sustainable growth.

What We Do

  • Critical Technical Fixes: Resolve crawl errors, implement schema markup, optimize Core Web Vitals, fix broken links
  • Site Architecture Optimization: Redesign URL structure if needed, implement strategic internal linking, optimize site hierarchy
  • Performance & Security: Compress images, optimize code, implement caching, ensure HTTPS properly configured

Expected Results

  • All critical technical issues resolved
  • Schema markup live across priority pages
  • Core Web Vitals improved by 30-50%
  • Technical foundation score: 90%+ (Lighthouse/PageSpeed Insights)
04
Weeks 4-12

Content Engine Launch

Timeline: 8 weeks (overlaps with Phase 3 & 5)

Strategic content production with topic cluster architecture, 32-55 SEO-optimized posts (depending on tier), comprehensive topical authority building.

What We Do

  • Strategic Content Production: Weeks 4-6: 8-15 foundational posts | Weeks 7-9: 12-20 cluster expansion posts | Weeks 10-12: 12-20 advanced content
  • Topic Cluster Architecture: Build 3-5 topic clusters targeting your core services/products
  • Content Components: Strategic keyword optimization, comprehensive research, expert-level writing (1,500-3,000 words), on-page optimization
  • Internal Linking: Strategic linking between pillar and cluster content

Expected Results

  • 32-55 SEO-optimized blog posts published (depending on tier)
  • 3-5 pillar pages established
  • Topic cluster internal linking complete
  • Content performance tracking active
05
Weeks 6-20

Authority Building

Timeline: Ongoing from Week 6

Strategic link acquisition through digital PR campaigns, high-authority outreach, broken link building, guest posting, and content partnerships.

What We Do

  • Digital PR Campaigns: Create data studies, original research, or industry reports that earn editorial links
  • Strategic Outreach: Target high-authority sites in your niche (DA 40+)
  • Broken Link Building: Reclaim competitor link equity through strategic replacement
  • Link Quality Focus: Domain authority, topical relevance, editorial placement, diversity, sustainability

Expected Results

  • Pro Tier: 5-10 high-quality backlinks/month (25-50 total in 5 months)
  • Enterprise Tier: 10-20 high-quality backlinks/month (50-100 total)
  • Backlink acquisition report (monthly)
  • Link gap analysis vs. competitors (quarterly)
06
Weeks 12-24

Optimization & Scaling

Timeline: Ongoing from Month 3

Performance analysis, content refresh, conversion optimization, and strategic scaling based on what's working.

What We Do

  • Performance Analysis: Identify top-performing content, analyze underperforming content, monitor competitor movements
  • Strategic Optimization: Content refresh (update underperforming posts), conversion optimization (test CTAs, lead magnets), internal linking expansion
  • Scaling Production: Increase content velocity based on results, expand into adjacent topic areas, launch new content formats

Expected Results

  • Monthly optimization reports (what we improved and why)
  • Refreshed/updated content (5-10 posts/month)
  • New content continues (adjusted pace based on performance)
  • Quarterly strategy reviews (strategic pivots if needed)
07
Months 6-12+

Market Domination

Timeline: Months 6-12 and beyond

Establish category leadership, expand geographically and vertically, sustain and compound results for long-term market position.

What We Do

  • Establish Category Leadership: Dominate priority keyword clusters (50+ page 1 rankings), own featured snippets, become the go-to resource in your niche
  • Expand & Diversify: Geographic expansion (local SEO, international SEO), vertical expansion (adjacent markets), format expansion (video, podcasts)
  • Sustain & Compound: Maintain content freshness, continue link building, monitor competitive threats, optimize for emerging search features

Expected Results

  • Market leadership position (top 3 in your category)
  • Sustainable organic traffic engine (1,000-10,000+ visitors/month)
  • Revenue attribution (clear ROI from SEO efforts)
  • Continued growth trajectory (traffic up 50-100% year-over-year)
